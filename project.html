COMP335 Final Group Project Idea

Recipe Finder

Our final project is a website that uses the ingredients you already have in your kitchen to come up with a recipe. The user enters the ingredients they have, like chicken, rice and onions, and the website gives back a recipe they can make with them.

The goal is to help people cook with what they already have instead of buying more groceries or letting food go to waste.
